The electronics, the code and the enclosure are all our work, so what arrives is a complete device rather than a bag of parts.

Custom hardware & PCB design

We start from the problem and work outwards — the circuit that solves it, the board that carries it, the firmware that runs it and the case that houses it. Every design is drawn for manufacture, so it can be milled on the bench for a prototype or sent to a partner fab for a run without redrawing a thing.

  • Schematic capture and circuit design
  • PCB layout, routing and design-for-manufacture
  • Embedded firmware, from board bring-up to over-the-air updates
  • Enclosures and mechanical design, ready to laser-cut or print

Purpose-built test & measurement

Some jobs need an instrument that doesn't exist yet — a jig to exercise a board, a bench to log a sensor for a week, a fixture to prove a part before it ships. We build the rig around the measurement rather than forcing the measurement to fit a rig, and wire it into the rest of your system so the results land somewhere you can actually use them. We build bespoke test equipment for clients across aviation, the yacht industry and education, among others.

  • Bench rigs and production test jigs
  • Data-logging and long-run soak testing
  • Sensor and signal characterisation
  • Bespoke fixtures and calibration setups
